About Whisper TTS

What is Whisper TTS?

Whisper TTS is a powerful text-to-speech service that converts your written text into natural-sounding speech. Whether you need voiceovers, accessibility features, or just want to hear your text read aloud, Whisper TTS provides high-quality audio generation in multiple languages and voices.

Features

  • Multiple TTS Engines: Choose between Google TTS (gTTS) for speed or Microsoft Edge TTS for quality
  • 12+ Languages: Support for English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, and Hindi
  • Voice Selection: Male and female voices with various accents and styles
  • Speed Control: Adjust speech rate from 0.5x (slow) to 2.0x (fast)
  • Instant Generation: Get your audio file in seconds
  • Downloadable MP3: Download and save your generated speech
  • Character Limit: Process up to 3000 characters per generation

TTS Engines

Google TTS (gTTS)

Fast and reliable text-to-speech powered by Google Translate's TTS API. Ideal for quick generations and when speed is a priority. Supports a wide range of languages with good quality output.

Microsoft Edge TTS

Premium neural voices from Microsoft Edge browser. Provides exceptionally natural-sounding speech with human-like intonation and emotion. Offers multiple voice options per language including male and female voices with various accents.

Privacy & Data

Generated audio files are automatically cleaned up after 24 hours. We do not permanently store your text or audio files. Your privacy is important to us.

Technical Details

  • Audio Format: MP3 (compressed, web-compatible)
  • Sample Rate: Varies by engine (typically 24kHz or 44.1kHz)
  • Processing Time: 2-5 seconds for most texts
  • File Size: Approximately 1-2MB per minute of audio
